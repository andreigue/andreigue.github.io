This blog is a personal thinking space. I use writing to slow ideas down, examine assumptions, and spend some quality time with thoughts before deciding what I believe. Most of what’s written here is not meant to persuade or perform certainty, but to give thoughts enough room to breathe and develop.

Ideas are allowed to change, mature, or be discarded entirely.

This blog is shared publicly, but it exists primarily for me.
